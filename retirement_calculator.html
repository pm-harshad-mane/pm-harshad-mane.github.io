Formula assumptions

1. Retirement monthly expense = current monthly expense × (1 + inflation)years to retirement × (1 − expense reduction).

2. Retirement corpus is calculated as the present value, at retirement, of monthly end-of-period withdrawals over the retirement years. Withdrawals are held at the retirement-start expense level, matching the simplest interpretation of NISM's stated retired-life return input.

3. Current retirement savings compound at the working-years return until retirement. Retirement benefits are added at retirement.

4. Required monthly investment uses an ordinary monthly annuity (contributions at month-end) to close the retirement funding gap.
